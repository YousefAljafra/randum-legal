randum - Privacy Policy

randum is a small personal app that posts AI-generated videos, approved by its owner, to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What data the app uses

  • Only the owner's own TikTok and Instagram login tokens, which are needed to upload videos. They are stored encrypted and are used for nothing else.
  • The app does not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or other users.
  • No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking.

Third-party services

Videos are uploaded through the official TikTok and Instagram APIs. Those platforms handle viewer data under their own privacy policies.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in their TikTok or Instagram settings, which makes the stored tokens useless.
