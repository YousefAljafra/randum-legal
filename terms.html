randum - Terms of Service

randum is a small personal app run by one person (the "owner"). It publishes short AI-generated animated videos with original fruit characters to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  • The app only posts to the owner's own accounts. It does not post for anyone else.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is uploaded.
  • Videos are clearly labelled as AI-generated content.

Use

The app is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up. The videos are entertainment only and are provided "as is", without warranties of any kind.
